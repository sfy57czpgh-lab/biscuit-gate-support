Unlock the Full Game

All 20 stages in CHICK are free. Full Game is a single, one-time in-app purchase that unlocks access to 240 additional stages across five worlds. It is not a subscription.

  1. On the world selection screen, select a world that requires Full Game to open the Full Game Unlock screen.
  2. A parent or guardian should review the displayed price and tap BUY.
  3. Complete the Parents Only gate, then follow Apple’s purchase confirmation steps.

The App Store displays the price for your region before you confirm. World progression requirements still apply after purchase: each world requires five different stage CLEARs in the previous world.

Restore purchases

If you previously purchased Full Game, use Restore Purchases to restore access without buying it again.

  1. Connect to the internet and make sure the App Store is signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
  2. Open SETTINGS and tap Restore Purchases. This button is also available on the Full Game Unlock screen.
  3. Ask a parent or guardian to complete the Parents Only gate, then follow any Apple prompts.

If no purchase is found, check the Apple Account used for the original purchase and try again. If the store is unavailable, try again later; the Full Game Unlock screen also offers Retry Store. Contact us if the problem continues.

Restoring a purchase restores Full Game access. It does not restore or transfer locally saved progress, plush toys, or settings.

For parents and guardians

BISCUIT GATE is a puzzle game designed for children ages 5 and up. A Parents Only gate asks an adult to solve a multiplication question before purchasing, restoring purchases, or opening the in-app Privacy Policy link. Children should ask a parent or guardian for help.

Completing the gate does not make a purchase by itself. Purchases also require Apple’s confirmation. Parents and guardians can manage purchase restrictions through Apple’s Screen Time settings.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a world still locked after I purchased Full Game?

Full Game provides access to the additional worlds, but you still need five different stage CLEARs in the previous world to progress. If your purchase is not recognized, try Restore Purchases.

Does the game have ads or require an account?

The game has no ads or third-party analytics. You do not need to create an account with us to play. Purchases and purchase restoration use Apple’s App Store.

Where are my progress and plush toys saved?

Your progress, plush toy collection, and settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app removes its local save data. Please do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step if you want to keep your progress. Purchase restoration does not recover this data.

What should I do if the game is not working?

Close and reopen the game, restart your device, and check the App Store for an available update. If the issue continues, email us with the details listed above.

How can I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem page to request help with a charge.
